## 2. WHY THE (M, D) PLANE, AND VERIFICATION BEFORE ANY CLAIM (P0)

The plane is forced by an **EXACT** identity, not chosen. For a candidate set `{X_1 … X_m}`
placed in a common frame with mean `A`, and any rigid map taking `A` onto the native `T`:

```
readout²  =  M²  −  D²    M² = (1/m) Σ RMSD_fixedframe(X_i, T)²   D² = (1/m) Σ RMSD(X_i, A)²
```

**Two recorded Sprint 16 errors are avoided by construction**, both in the brief's §5 table:
`M` is the **common-frame** member error, never the free-superposition RMSD (that substitution
cost an attribution ≈2×), and every mean is **quadratic**, never arithmetic (that substitution
cost 54 % of a "prediction error"). `M_free` is computed and printed beside `M` on every set;
at m = 75 they differ by ≈0.2 Å, so the distinction is not academic.

| check | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| identity on 12 random sets, m = 3…80, two register sizes | max abs residual **5.33e−15 Å²** | PASS |
| identity across all 340 aggregated arm points in the report | max abs residual **2.40e−14 Å²** | PASS |
| `prior` exactly additive over residues | max residual **9.69e−07** = the float32 spacing | PASS |
| the new fixed-T Metropolis thermostat respects its budget | **8,192 / 8,192** | PASS |
| fast Walsh–Hadamard round-trip; Parseval | **8.9e−16**; **0.0** | PASS |
| `mps2f` represents a computational basis state exactly | max prob **1.000000000000**; 64/64 random targets reached 64 distinct basis states | PASS |
| instrument reproduces Sprint 16 | certified argmin **2.661 Å**, space best **1.030 Å** — identical to `s16/qphase_FINDINGS.md` | PASS |
| seeds | `SD.stable_rng`, salt `s17quantum`; `hash()` nowhere | PASS |

**A sign error in my own primary statistic, caught before publication and recorded rather than
quietly fixed.** The first draft of `dominance_margin` computed `min_c (M_p − M_c)`, which
equals `M_p − max_c M_c` — the *worst* classical point — and it scored every VQE arm as a
large win (−0.48 to −0.89 Å, CIs excluding zero). The correct quantity is `M_p − min_c M_c`.
It was found by hand-checking a printed table against the stated rule.

**A second, subtler defect in the same statistic, and the fix.** `M_p − min{M_c : D_c ≥ D_p −
τ}` is **undefined** when the VQE reaches a diversity no classical arm reaches — and that case
is not missing data, it is exactly the case in which the VQE point *cannot* be dominated.
Averaging over the defined cells would have silently deleted the only cells capable of being
quantum wins. The primary statistic is therefore the **additive ε-dominance indicator**

```
eps(p) = min over classical c of  max( M_c − M_p , D_p − D_c )      [Angstroms]
eps > 0  ⇒  NO classical point dominates p  ⇒  a quantum win
```

defined on every target, with the correct sign there by construction. The conditional
M-margin is retained as a secondary and its undefined count is printed.

## 3. THE ARMS, AND WHAT COUNTS AS CLASSICAL (P2)

**The classical control Sprint 16 never ran.** Its thermostats were `tilt_samples` (a reweight
of a drawn multiset — structurally unable to leave its own support) and `tilt_exact` (reads
the whole register; measured here at **318,318** objective evaluations, 39× the budget).

a fixed-temperature single-residue Metropolis chain with restarts, budget-capped by the same
`Counter` every other arm uses, swept over a **nine-point temperature ladder in units of the
objective's own sd**, so the classical arm traces a **curve**. A single temperature is a
point, and a point cannot be a frontier.

**Classical attainable set (21 points, all at or below the matched 8,192-evaluation budget,
none touching a circuit):** `metro_T` × 9 · `metro1opt_T` × 3 (the falsifier's named
"thermostat plus 1-opt") · `greedy` · `anneal` · `anneal_q` (¼ budget) · `random` ·
`retrieval_T` × 5 (the retrieval torsion prior's own Boltzmann product law, at **zero** reads
of the deployed objective). §7 adds three more: the identical circuit with the CNOTs removed.

**Reported but never counted as classical:** `untrained`, `tilt_samples`, `tilt_exact`.

**Quantum arms (10):** CVaR-VQE at α ∈ {0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 1.00} (α = 1 is plain
VQE), two α schedules (high→low, low→high), a **retrieval-informed warm start**
(`init="prior"` from the exactly-additive retrieval prior's marginals), and a deeper ansatz
(`mps3f`). §7 adds a shots ladder at fixed budget and restarts.

**Every arm reports every cost axis** — objective evaluations · circuit samples · gradient
passes · distinct configurations · wall clock · CPU seconds — and the cost columns are printed

**Two consumption modes, both reported, never substituted.** `M/D` — a set drawn *uniformly*

pipeline consumes. **The two give different answers**, and reporting either alone would
mislead; this is the same lesson as Sprint 16's §3 (argmin and ensemble readouts have opposite
quality dependence).

## 6. ENSEMBLE REWEIGHTING AND α-AS-A-TEMPERATURE (P4)

`python -m s17.q_repr run`. **n = 8 targets × 2 seeds** (see §10). The one thing a trained
circuit owns that no classical search does is a normalised distribution `q_θ` defined on the

### 6a. α is a temperature — and the exact version of that claim is weaker than the correlational one

Sprint 16 established α-as-temperature by a correlation over six points (Pearson +0.93/+0.98).
The exact version: fit `T_eff` minimising `KL(q_θ ‖ Boltzmann(E, T))` and report the residual.

| α | H(q_θ) bits | T_eff (Boltzmann) | **KL bits** | T_eff (p0-tilted) | KL bits | KL(q_θ ‖ p0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.02 | 11.46 | 0.668 | **5.10** | 0.678 | 9.85 | 11.32 |
| 0.10 | 9.57 | 0.498 | 5.99 | 0.469 | 10.25 | 12.74 |
| 0.25 | 7.84 | 0.350 | 5.76 | 0.315 | 8.94 | 13.53 |
| 1.00 | 5.14 | 0.190 | **4.55** | 0.161 | 6.71 | 15.65 |

**`T_eff` falls monotonically with α — 0.668 → 0.190 — which is the temperature claim, exactly
confirmed and now in physical units rather than as a correlation.** But the residual KL is
**4.55–5.99 bits** against a distribution whose own entropy is 5.14–11.46 bits, i.e. the
misfit is 40–90 % of the distribution's entropy. So `q_θ` is **not** a Boltzmann law of `E`;
it is a distribution whose *concentration* is set by a temperature that α controls. The
correct statement is **"α sets a temperature", not "q_θ is that Boltzmann law"** — SUPPORTED,
and a genuine refinement of Sprint 16's I9, which the correlational method could not have
distinguished. The `p0`-tilted family fits *worse* at every α, so the trained state moves away
from its own initialisation's shape rather than tilting it.

**The deployed objective is a separable per-residue field to 93 % of its variance, and its
separable part alone has a better optimum than the whole of it.** A separable objective's
global minimiser is a closed-form per-residue argmin; the measured budget floor in §0 is the
operational consequence. Meanwhile the *answer* lives at mean Pauli weight 3.72 with half its
pairwise mass between residues. **The mismatch is between objective and truth, not between
classical and quantum.** — EXACT (the spectrum), ORACLE (the RMSD columns).

A corollary worth stating because it inverts the usual quantum-advantage argument: the regime
in which a variational quantum sampler would have a *structural* case is one where the
objective is high-degree and frustrated. Improving this objective toward the truth would move
it in exactly that direction — and a bounded-correlation ansatz would then be *less* able to
represent its Boltzmann law, not more. PLAUSIBLE, stated as a direction and not measured.

### 9b. The representability theorem, and what it does to the Pareto framing — EXACT

The `mps2f` family contains **every computational basis state exactly**: setting the final RY
layer's angles to 0 or π and the rest to 0 gives a product basis state, and the CNOT chain maps
basis states to basis states. Verified: max probability **1.000000000000** on every trial, and
64 random targets reached 64 *distinct* basis states.

**Consequence.** The unbudgeted variational optimum of `CVaR_α`, for *any* α, is a delta on the
objective's certified argmin — diversity exactly zero. **Therefore every VQE point with D > 0
is a partially converged intermediate, not a designed frontier point.** Its position on the
(member error, diversity) plane is a coordinate on a collapse trajectory whose endpoint is
fixed by the objective and reached exactly by greedy 1-opt at 1,024 evaluations. That is why
the α ladder in §4a is monotone in diversity with flat member error, and it is why "a
CVaR-VQE occupying an otherwise unreachable Pareto point" was never a well-posed hope: the
optimiser is not aiming at any point on that plane except the corner.

### 9c. What is NOT claimed

No barren-plateau claim is made: two register sizes are not a scaling law, and Sprint 16
already showed the run is budget-limited rather than plateau-limited (more optimisation is
better). The gradient-estimator signal-to-noise measurement (`q_theory grad`) was written but
not run under the compute constraint; `core.quantum`'s own recorded cosine of +0.994 for the
sampled `const`-baseline estimator against the exact reference stands unchallenged and is the
reason the failure is called representational rather than statistical.

## 12. WHAT THIS MEANS

**For the quantum pillar.** The falsifier this workstream pre-registered has fired on every
one of its clauses. The pillar has no accuracy role in this architecture and should be
retained as a studied object only. The two untested reformulations the sprint identified as
most promising — local VQE and ensemble reweighting — are both closed, and each is closed by a
*classical* control that is cheaper than the quantum arm rather than by a bad quantum result.

**For the rest of the sprint, which is where the value is.** Two measurements here are not
about quantum at all and should be used:

1. **The deployed objective is a separable per-residue field to 93 % of its variance, with 96 %
   of its pairwise coupling intra-residue, while the truth has mean Pauli weight 3.72 with half
   its pairwise mass between residues.** Any effort to improve the objective should be measured
   on whether it adds *inter-residue* structure, because that is the entire content of the gap.
   The degree-1 truncation's optimum being *better* than the full objective's (2.411 vs
   2.661 Å) says the objective's existing higher-degree content is not merely insufficient — on
   this instrument it is pointing the wrong way.
2. **Local refinement of a retrieval candidate is worth ~0.7 Å at the argmin readout** (3.636 →
   2.95 Å over 6-residue windows), it is certified by exhaustive enumeration at 4,096
   evaluations per window, and greedy reaches the same optimum at 1,024. That is a cheap,
   exact, native-free operator the selection lane can use today.

**And a methodological one for the programme's ledger.** Before comparing two search methods
at a "matched budget", measure the budget the *control* actually needs. Sprints 15–17 spent
three sprints comparing arms at 8× past saturation, and none of the four audits caught it,
because every number in every comparison was correct.
