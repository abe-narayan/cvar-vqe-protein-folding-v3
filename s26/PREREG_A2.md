## The object

The deployed circuit is U = C R_L ... C R_1 |0...0>, R_l = prod_q exp(-i theta_{l,q} Y_q / 2),
C = CNOT chain plus ring (`core/quantum.py` `StatevectorCircuit`, ring=True, layers=3, n=7).
Moving every C to the right, theta_{l,q} generates the single Pauli string
P_{l,q} = C^(L-l+1) Y_q C^-(L-l+1) (C is Clifford) and a fixed basis permutation C^L trails.
The DLA is the real Lie closure of {-i P_{l,q} / 2}. The closure is computed exactly on Pauli
strings as a set (bit vectors; anticommutation = odd symplectic product; commutator string =
bitwise product; BFS over "commute with a generator"; distinct strings are linearly
independent and right-normed nested commutators span the algebra). Cap 2^21 = 2,097,152
strings, the smallest power of two that holds dim so(2^11) = 2,096,128. Cross-check at n = 4
and 5: dense matrices -iP/2, nested commutators, SVD rank, relative tolerance 1e-10.
Code: `s26/q_dla.py`; tests `s26/q_tests.py` (symbolic == numeric at n = 3, 4 for L = 1..3;
left and right conjugation conventions agree; conjugated strings equal the dense conjugates up
to sign; the closure of the pools stays in the odd-Y set).

## Expected outcome, and what it says about S25's slopes

If dim(DLA) at the deployed depth is exponential in n (the prediction: su(2^(n-1)) or
so(2^n)), then Larocca et al. (Quantum 6, 824, 2022) and Ragone et al. (Nat. Commun. 15, 7172,
2024) give Var[dC/dtheta] ~ 1/dim(g) ONLY for circuits that form 2-designs over exp(g). At
P = 3n = 21 against dim(g) >= 4095 the deployed circuit is nowhere near that regime, so the
S25 slopes (-0.65 to -0.05 log2 per qubit against the -1 of a 2-design measured by S13 at
depth 8, `s13/results/geo_kernel.json`) are a statement about SHALLOWNESS at fixed depth, not
about the algebra. If instead dim(DLA) turned out polynomial in n, the absence of a plateau
would be explained by the algebra itself (Larocca 2022, Prop. on poly-size DLAs) and would
also, by Cerezo et al. 2025 (arXiv:2312.09121), place the circuit in the classically simulable
class. Either way the register is 7 qubits and every state is simulated exactly; no claim of
advantage follows from any value of dim(DLA).
