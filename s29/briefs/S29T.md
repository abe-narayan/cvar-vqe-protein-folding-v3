## Deliverable: `s29/THEORY.md`, one section per question, each ending in a measurable prediction
1. THE CONTRACTION THEOREM. Let the posterior over each pair distance be p_ij with median
   m_ij and the pool members W_k with distances d_ij(W_k). Show exactly why the L1 Bayes risk
   S(C) = sum_ij w_ij E_p|t - d_ij(C)| over structures C is minimised near the per-pair median
   map, why a coordinate average of members has distances BELOW the members' mean distances
   (Jensen on the norm; quantify the 22% contraction as a function of the members' spread),
   and under what condition the average's cost is lower than every member's (S28-L36: DIS
   puts production at the 12.6th percentile of its own pool). Derive how a 2x over-confident
   posterior (S25 L2) changes the minimiser. Prediction: the sign of dS/d(scale) at production
   as a function of over-confidence, checkable on 12 targets.
2. THE LOCALLY INFORMATIVE CLASS. Given only per-pair marginals p_ij (no joint), characterise
   the objectives f(C) whose gradient at the production cloud has positive expected cosine
   with the direction to the native, under the record's error model: the native's deviation
   from production is 68% common-mode across members (S23 L9) and the posterior's error is
   coherent with the pool's (S18/S19: every predictor emits the typical peptide). Show whether
   ANY function of the marginals alone can have positive cosine when the marginals' error is
   parallel to the pool's error, and what an objective would need to see (a second, differently
   biased source? the pool's own dispersion? a joint?) for the cosine to leave the random
   reference. This is the theorem behind finding 8; state it as a theorem with its
   assumptions, and say which assumption a new information source would have to break.
3. THE SPECTRAL CONDITION FOR A MEANINGFUL NON-DIAGONAL HAMILTONIAN. S28 found the Gaussian
   similarity graph is near rank one (Perron vector 95 to 99% uniform) and that the hopping
   term's gradient variance decays with the number of qubits because of the SPECTRUM, not the
   off-diagonality. Derive: (a) for A = exp(-d^2/2 sigma^2) on a pool whose pairwise RMSDs sit
   in a band [d_min, d_max] with d_max/sigma small, the ratio lambda_2/lambda_1 as a function
   of the band (why it is 0.11 to 0.14 here); (b) the gradient variance of <psi|A|psi> for the
   RY/CNOT ansatz as a function of the spectrum of A (the S28 rank-one decomposition
   generalised: express Var[dF/dtheta] in terms of the eigenvalues); (c) the centered
   compatibility matrix A_c = A - (1/N) 1 1^T A - ... (double centering) and the signed
   agreement matrix (deviation-from-mean inner products, D D^T with D the members' deviations
   from the pool mean in the medoid frame): their spectra on 12 real pools, and whether the
   gradient-variance decay is then flat in n; (d) what the ground state of diag(E) - J A_c
   IS (a coherent set with mutually aligned deviations) and why that is or is not the
   consistency mechanism S27 section 6 measured as harmful. Prediction: the gradient-variance
   slope for the centered graph at n = 4..9, to be measured by lane B when it exists.
4. CVaR OVER A STRUCTURAL OBSERVABLE. The deployed CVaR acts on a scalar energy per basis
   state. Formalise CVaR_alpha over a vector-valued or set-valued observable (the distance map

5. THE REACHABLE SET. For the deployed ansatz (RY on every qubit, CNOT ladder, depth 3, real
