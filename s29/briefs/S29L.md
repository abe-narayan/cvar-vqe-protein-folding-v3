## Topics, in the order the sprint needs them
1. NATIVE-FREE STRUCTURE QUALITY (finding 8, the deepest). Model quality assessment without
   the native: single-model QA (ProQ3/ProQ4, VoroMQA, DeepAccNet, QMEAN/QMEANDisCo, GraphQA,
   EnQA, the CASP QA assessments and what won), consensus QA (Pcons, DAVIS-QAconsensus) and its
   known failure under correlated decoys, and the peptide-specific literature (short peptides
   have almost no tertiary contacts; what QA works below 20 residues?). For each: what signal
   it uses, whether that signal is in our library (S27's 18 channels, RAMA, DSSPHB, LEG, AMB;
   S28-L48's 31 scorers all failed), and whether it could be trained leave-fold-out on our
   corpus without the native of the target. The question to answer: is there ANY native-free
   quality signal for a 9 to 16-mer that the field has shown to work, and on what data?
2. CORRELATED ERROR IN ENSEMBLES AND METHODS THAT BREAK IT (finding 11): ensemble methods
   under common-mode error, bias-variance under correlated members, "diverse ensembles",
   negative correlation learning, boosting-style residual fitting, control variates,
   two-source contrasts (the Sprint's H1 idea: extrapolation between two differently biased
   estimators, e.g. Richardson-style or bias-cancelling combinations), and systematic-bias
   correction in structure prediction (recycling, refinement against a physics term).
3. DECISION THEORY OF STRUCTURE POINT ESTIMATES: Bayes risk under L1/L2 in coordinate vs
   distance space, medoid vs mean under multimodality, proper scoring rules for distograms,
   calibration of distance posteriors (what AlphaFold/trRosetta/RoseTTAFold did about
   over-confidence; temperature scaling for distograms), and any result that the Bayes
   estimator of an over-confident posterior is contracted.
4. QUANTUM (charter section 6, first group): CVaR-VQE (Barkoutsos 2020) and the later
   risk-sensitive variants; QAOA's mixer as the meaning of an off-diagonal term; ADAPT-VQE and
   operator pools; dynamical Lie algebras and trainability (Larocca et al.), cost-function
   locality and barren plateaus (Cerezo et al.), the CVaR gradient's properties; variational
   Gibbs / thermal states; quantum Boltzmann machines; ansatz reachability. For each: the
   condition under which the quantum object differs from its classical counterpart (the set-
   equality theorem here says the CVaR tail of a diagonal H is a classical prefix), and what
   Hamiltonian structure would make the variational state NOT classically reproducible by a
   sort or an eigensolver. Name the papers that show a variational state doing something an
   eigensolver on the same H cannot, and under what conditions.
5. PEPTIDE STRUCTURE FROM SEQUENCE AT 9 TO 16 RESIDUES: what accuracy the field reaches with
   fragment assembly (Rosetta ab initio, PEP-FOLD, PEPstrMOD), with language-model methods
   (ESMFold, OmegaFold on peptides), with MD (folding@home-scale studies of designed
   peptides), and which of those inputs is legitimately available here (CPU, no structure-
   trained predictor because of leakage against the natives). What is the published ceiling
   for native-free peptide prediction at this length, and how does 3.21 A compare?
