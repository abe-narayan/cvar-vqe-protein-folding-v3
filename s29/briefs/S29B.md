## The new angle, stated up front (contract rule 10)
S28 closed "the Gaussian similarity graph's hopping term is gradient-invisible at the deployed
width" and traced it to the SPECTRUM: A = exp(-d^2/2 sigma^2) is near rank one (lambda_2/lambda_1
0.138 at n = 9; Perron vector 95 to 99% uniform), so <psi|A|psi> is essentially the squared
overlap with one fixed near-uniform vector. Lane T has now measured, on 12 real pools, that this
is a property of the RAW similarity and not of off-diagonality: double-centering the same matrix
gives lambda_2/lambda_1 = 0.465 at n = 9, and the signed AGREEMENT matrix G = D D^T (D the
members' deviations from the pool mean in the medoid frame, i.e. the common-mode-removed
structure) gives 0.634. The closure was about one degenerate similarity measure. This lane asks
the question S28 could not: with a non-degenerate off-diagonal term whose eigenvectors carry
structure, does the CVaR-VQE's state, and the structure it emits, change?

## What the Hamiltonian means (charter section 11, answer all nine in the prereg)
H = diag(E) - J M, E the deployed rank ladder over the pool, M one of:
- A_c: the double-centered Gaussian similarity (the common mode removed; its top eigenvectors
  are the pool's principal modes of DISAGREEMENT).
- G: the signed agreement matrix of deviations from the pool mean, normalised to unit spectral
  norm. <psi|G|psi> is large when the state's members' deviations from the typical structure
  are mutually ALIGNED: a coherent hypothesis about which way this target departs from typical.
  This is the charter's "Hamiltonian encoding the disagreement between the prior and the pool"
  and the record's 68% common-mode error entering as the thing that is REMOVED rather than
  averaged in.
- (optional third) G_res: the same with each member's deviation first residualised on the
  posterior's own predicted deviation, so the term sees only what the posterior did not.
Say in the prereg what a basis state means, what M's eigenvectors are structurally, and why a
coherent set along a principal disagreement mode should be better OR worse than the average
(S27 section 6 says consistency mechanisms have been harmful; state your prior accordingly).
