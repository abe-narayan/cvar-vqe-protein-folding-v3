## The question, and why it is not S23 L6
Production's cloud is 3.0483 and its built chain is 3.2126: the projection COSTS +0.159 A. The
cloud is contracted about 22% (mean virtual CA-CA bond 2.96 A against the ideal 3.8). S23 L6
closed the scale s* that MINIMISES RMSD: it needs the native, it is a property of the (pool,
native) pair, and a global scalar captures 1.7% of its ceiling. This lane asks a different
question with a fully determined, native-free answer: the cloud is geometrically INCONSISTENT
(its own bonds are not 3.8 A), the projection's job is to find the nearest ideal-geometry chain,
and a least-squares fit to a systematically contracted cloud is a biased fit. Does removing the
geometric inconsistency BEFORE the projection change the built chain? The rescale factor is
read from the cloud's own bonds, never from the native, so the S23 L6 impossibility does not
apply; state that distinction in the prereg and cite L6 by line.
