## The question

given the pool as it is? The S28 ladder (uniform top-75 average 2.954 point cloud; best single
member 2.306 / 1.711; convex hull 0.853; the deployed circuit family 0.252 emitted) mixes
classes with very different recognisability. The sprint needs the ceiling of each class a

say "the ceiling is imposed by X" with a number. Pre-register in `s29/PREREG_S29_O.md` (the
list below, the basis, the projection budget) before the first number.
