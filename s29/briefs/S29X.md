## The question
Under the deployed encoding a basis state is a candidate ID and the Hamiltonian is diagonal, so
the CVaR tail is a classical prefix (S28-L21) and the quantum stage can be deleted. Is there a
state space in which the quantum state represents a DISTRIBUTION OVER STRUCTURAL HYPOTHESES
that the pool average cannot represent, with an interacting Hamiltonian whose ground state is
genuinely correlated, such that the tail ENSEMBLE (not the argmin) emits a structure and
removing the quantum stage degrades it?

## The formulation to design and probe (you may change it; say why)
- Basis states: per-residue structural hypotheses. Two candidate encodings: (i) each residue's
  (phi, psi) in B bins (B = 4 or 8 from the per-fold Ramachandran table `s8/generate_rama.npz`,
  the S13 encoding); (ii) each residue's FRAGMENT choice among F local fragments cut from the
  top-75 pool members (F = 4 or 8), so the state space is combinatorial recombination of pool
  members (a configuration is a chimera of members; the pool average is NOT in this space, and
  neither is any single member's contraction). Say which you choose first and why; (ii) is
  the one the record has never run.
- Hamiltonian: H = sum over pairs of the posterior's negative log-probability of the realised
  distance (a 2-body term in the configuration variables; the S13 locality theorem says d_ij
  depends on the residues between i and j, so it is (j-i)-local, not 2-local, and you must
  say how you evaluate it: exact on the built configuration is fine for n <= 16), plus the
  per-fold torsion prior as 1-body terms, plus a transverse-field mixer as the off-diagonal
  term (the QAOA meaning: transitions between neighbouring hypotheses). The ground state of the
  diagonal part is the S13 certified optimum (which ties the pool); the mixer is what makes it
  non-classical; whether the mixer's ground state is a useful superposition is the question.
- Objective: CVaR_alpha over the sampled configurations' H, with the entropy term, seeds 0/1.
- Readout: the coordinate average of the CVaR tail's configurations (each configuration
  built to a CA trace with ideal geometry); then the production projection. This is the
  ensemble readout that S13 to S21 never used (they used the argmin).
- Controls (pre-registered): the diagonal H's exact argmin (S21's enumeration; the classical
  control); simulated annealing over the same H at matched evaluations, tail-averaged the same
  way; the untrained circuit's tail; a permuted posterior; the production average.
- The probe: 12 targets (S27's trainability set), n up to 16 residues x log2(F) qubits, so
  choose F and the qubit count to fit an exact statevector (n_qubits <= 20 is 8 MB of complex
  amplitudes per state; fine) and quote the peak RSS; point cloud and built chain; every
  contrast with `ST.fmt`; then, only if any arm clears 0.7x MDE on 12 targets against
  production, the 126.
