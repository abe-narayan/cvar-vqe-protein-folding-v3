## 2. THE THEORY — why this was structural, and it damages my own hypothesis hardest

### T1 — the CVaR indifference theorem — **EXACT**, verified

Let `E : Z → R` with `E* = min E` on `Z*`, and `CVaR_α(p)` the mean of the lowest α-fraction of
`E` under `p`. Then `CVaR_α(p) ≥ E*` with **equality iff `p(Z*) ≥ α`** — so the minimiser is the
**face** `{p : p(Z*) ≥ α}`, on which everything `p` does with its remaining `1−α` mass is **free**.

> **CVaR does not design a population. It constrains an α-tail and is indifferent to the rest.**

This generalises Sprint 17 §9b to *any* family and *any* representation — and it does so **without
the representability premise, which is exactly the premise that fails here**: a von Mises
component with `κ` capped at 40 cannot be a delta, so Sprint 17's delta-collapse argument does not
port to continuous torsion space. T1 replaces it with a stronger statement that needs no such
premise.

Verified on 12 targets over 4,096 continuous configurations each (`qb_theory t1`). Three laws, all
**exactly** CVaR-optimal at the same α, and what they emit:

```
alpha = 0.25    worst-case CVaR spread over targets = 0.000e+00
   A_delta          D 0.000   set best 2.836   set mean 2.836   coord-avg 2.836
   B_uniform_tail   D 3.159   set best 2.236   set mean 3.957   coord-avg 2.831
   C_worst_tail     D 3.538   set best 2.509   set mean 4.436   coord-avg 3.193
```

**At machine-precision-identical, exactly optimal CVaR the emitted diversity spans 0.00→3.54 Å,
the generation ceiling spans 0.60 Å and the coordinate average spans 0.36 Å** (at α = 0.05 the
coordinate-average spread reaches 1.96 Å on 1DJF). The objective cannot see the difference.

**Restricted-family corollary (T1b, EXACT):** inside a family `{p_θ}` the minimiser need not be
degenerate, but `CVaR_α(p_θ)` is a functional of the **lower α-tail of the pushforward of `p_θ`
under `E`, and of nothing else** — hence blind to diversity, coverage, the set mean, and RMSD.
This is why "a CVaR-VQE occupying an otherwise unreachable point on the
(member error, diversity, coverage) frontier" was never a well-posed hope. `qb_theory t2` (the
numerical version of this over random parameters) was **NOT RUN** under the compute constraint.

### T3 — the latent bandwidth ceiling — **SUPPORTED**, n = 16

Any quantum sampler over a continuous space acts through a discrete latent. Decomposing
`Var = Var_b(E[·|b]) + E_b(Var(·|b))` with 64 continuous draws per latent:

```
between-latent share of Var(E)            mean 0.53   (range 0.26 - 0.80)
between-latent share of Var(CA-RMSD)      mean 0.27   (range 0.12 - 0.51)   ORACLE
rho(latent mean E, latent mean RMSD)      mean +0.49  (negative on 2/16 targets)
```

> **The latent the circuit controls carries about half the objective's variance and about a
> quarter of the structure's. Three quarters of the structural variation is in the continuous
> draw, which `p_θ` cannot touch.** This ceiling binds the CNOT-free and classical-chain latents
> identically — it is a property of the construction, not of quantumness, and is reported as such.

### T4 — the model class, and why no separation is available at this depth — **EXACT** + measured

`core.quantum.MPSAnsatz(n, layers=2, entangler="cnot")` has, by its own construction and its
`chi` attribute, **exactly bond dimension 4 with no truncation**. So `p_θ(b) = |ψ(b)|²` is a
matrix-product distribution of bond dimension ≤ 16 — a hidden Markov model with ≤ 16 latent
states, exactly normalisable and exactly samplable classically in `O(n·χ²)`.

> **There is therefore no sampling separation available from the deployed ansatz at any budget, by
> construction and not by measurement.** What the entangler can buy is a richer *correlation
> structure* over the latent than a product model has — a modelling question, not a quantum one,
> and exactly what `c_chain` tests classically.

Whether the circuit even uses that capacity (`qb_theory t4`, 16 targets, total correlation
`Σ_i H(b_i) − H(b)`):

```
trained + CNOT      1.328 bits total   0.106 bits/residue   entropy 5.45 bits
trained, no CNOT    0.000 bits total   0.000 bits/residue   entropy 4.11 bits
UNTRAINED + CNOT    1.524 bits total   0.123 bits/residue   entropy 9.31 bits
```

**Training does not build latent correlation — it destroys some.** The untrained circuit carries
*more* total correlation than the trained one. Nearest-neighbour latent MI at n = 126 is +0.051
bits trained against +0.047 untrained. The one thing an entangled ansatz is for is ~0.1 bits per
residue, present at initialisation, and unused by the objective.

2. **What I *can* say from what is on disk points the same way as Agent A's 0.87.** The samplers
   in this study draw their torsions from **von Mises mixtures fitted to the BLOSUM retrieval

   A generator built on the pool's marginals is the least likely thing to decorrelate from the
   pool's error. Any future test of VQE-as-decorrelating-generator must therefore use a candidate
   space that is **not** fitted to the retrieval pool, or it is answering a foregone question.
   This is a prediction, not a measurement: **PLAUSIBLE**.

The measurement itself is cheap — one rerun of two or three arms with the emitted sets retained,
then the same coherent-error statistic Agent A used, against the pool's. It is the first thing I
