The brief asked for real movement on the built-chain RMSD, or a real quantum result, or both, with the CVaR-VQE kept as the spine and everything around it fair game. The sprint built the three formulations the record had never tried and one second wave behind each, measured every arm on the built chain against production with the full control set, and had lane D attack every entry within the hour.

Every deployable arm of every formulation is inside its own MDE of production or worse than production beyond it. Every one of those verdicts survived the Adversary. What the sprint bought is the mechanism, measured on both sides of the pipeline:

  • Expressivity is not the barrier. The deployed circuit family, read as signed affine weights over the pool, holds a structure within 0.25 Å of the native on every one of the 126 targets ORACLE (S28-L26b).
  • Optimisation is not the barrier. The optimiser reaches the objective's optimum: λ = 0 reproduces the production VQE bit for bit, and the objective falls on 126 of 126 targets (S28-L18b, L26b).
  • The objective is the barrier, three ways. Its minimiser is away from the native (every signed arm worse, S28-L26b). Its gradient at the pipeline's own output has no directional information about the native (cosine −0.03, inside the random null, S28-L23b), and a step along it is worth a random step (S28-L39). And 20 of 31 native-free scorers in the whole library prefer the projected production average to a 0.25 Å structure (S28-L48).
  • The quantum side. The first non-diagonal Hamiltonian in the project was built, differentiated exactly and run end to end. Its hopping term is invisible to the circuit's gradient at the deployed width because the pool graph is a near-rank-one typicality projector (S28-L8b, L11). A spread-spectrum graph halves that decay; the circuit then finds the coherent basin on 25 to 39% of cells, and the emitted structure does not move on either class (S28-L46, L47).

2. Why cost reduction does not mean RMSD reduction

This is the sprint's central measurement and the reason every route died at the same place. The deployed cost is the L1 Bayes-risk of the shipped distogram against a structure's own distance map. Along the ladder that matters, from the circuit's optimum through production to the ORACLE structure and the native, the cost falls as RMSD rises.

The mechanism in the record's own numbers

The shipped posterior is about twice over-confident (S25 L2). The L1 Bayes-risk of an over-confident posterior is minimised by a structure whose distances sit at the posterior medians, and the pool's coordinate average is exactly that structure: it is contracted 22% against the native (mean virtual bond 2.96 Å vs 3.77) and it is the posterior's own fixed point. S29 correction “Contracted” is the wrong shape. S29 proved the distortion is an exact variance identity, d(C)² = ⟨dk²⟩ − s², and measured it as a separation-dependent shear that crosses 1.0 near |i−j| = 8 — short-range distances are compressed while long-range ones are over-extended. A uniform rescaling therefore cannot fix it, which is why item 3 below is closed and why pure de-contraction scores the worst signed cosine of all 21 displacement fields S29 measured (−0.0208, below a random shape field). Under the shipped cost, production ranks at the 12.6th percentile of its own pool: the cost prefers the contracted average to 87% of the real protein windows it was averaged from (S28-L35, L36).

So "reduce the cost" means "agree more with the posterior median", which means "contract toward the average", which is a direction that on most targets is orthogonal to the native and on the failing targets points away from it. The pool's error is 68% common-mode (S23 L9) and the distogram shares that error, because it trained on the same fragments; a cost built from it cannot see the error it shares.

The same failure is not specific to the distogram. Section 3.7 measures every native-free scorer in the library on the same ladder: 20 of 31 prefer the contracted production average to the 0.25 Å structure, and the one that clears both registered clauses is at the mean of the multiplicity null. The library was built around the same posterior and the same pool, and it inherits the same blind spot.

6. Did the quantum component earn anything?

No accuracy, and it was given every chance: its state was read as signed weights, the one readout that could have escaped the convex bottleneck; it was handed the first non-diagonal Hamiltonian in the project's history with an exact hopping gradient; and that Hamiltonian was re-built on a spread-spectrum graph at the one cell the mechanism licensed. On the built chain every deployable arm is inside its MDE of production or worse, every verdict survived the Adversary, and the classical spectral counterpart, the exact ground state, ties production wherever the circuit does.

What it earned is three measured properties that stand on their own and were not known before. The pool's similarity graph is a near-rank-one typicality projector whose hopping term decays at −1.7 to −1.8 per qubit and is 7,300× below the diagonal terms at the deployed width (S28-L8b, L11). The CVaR tail read from a non-diagonal H is still exactly the classical top-m prefix, to 1e−13, at every J (S28-L21): the set-equality theorem surviving its first genuine test. And under a spread spectrum the variational optimiser is bimodal, finding the coherent basin or not on a per-cell basis with nothing between (S28-L41, L46). The first two say why the circuit cannot matter through this readout; the third is a landscape fact about the ansatz that a future non-diagonal formulation would have to design around.

The quantum component's ceiling in this pipeline was measured to be the objective's ceiling, and the objective's ceiling is zero.

8. What to work on next: making cost reduction mean RMSD reduction

The goal is a cost whose reduction correlates with reduced RMSD in the region the optimiser explores. Today the correlation is negative (Figure 1: Spearman −0.40 along the ladder for the shipped cost, s28_C2_chain_summary.json :: ladder_rho), the gradient at production is blind (Figure 2), and the native sits at the cost's 37th percentile. The programme below is ordered by dependency and by cost. The first item is not optional: without a meter that reports the correlation, no experiment can claim to have improved it.

The pool-residual prior: train the cost to predict where the pool is wrong

The steep lever. The pool's error is 68% common-mode (S23 L9): on a given target most members are wrong in the same direction, and the distogram shares that direction because it was trained on the same fragments. A per-target correction therefore has most of the error variance to work with, and the prior's derivative is the only steep lever in the record (−2.15 Å per unit toward a perfect prior, S23). Train, leave-fold-out with the pinned folds, a head on ESM-2 features that predicts the pool's residual (realised minus predicted distance, per pair, sign and magnitude); the cost becomes the Bayes-risk against the corrected posterior. Two disciplines from the record are mandatory: the corrector's features must not be the predictor's own outputs (S19: a corrector trained on the predictor's features inherits its error structure; measure the coherence of its mistakes and require it to be i.i.d.-like), and the leakage-safe universe s8/generate_univ is the only training pool.

The C2 audit shows the existing library cannot tell a 0.25 Å structure from production. A cost trained directly on (structure, RMSD) pairs from the ORACLE ladders of the other folds' targets, using only features computed on the candidate itself (ideal-geometry backbone terms, contact statistics, torsion distributions; nothing from the pool), tested on held-out folds with the meter, is the direct test of whether nativeness is recognisable at all from a single peptide structure. The order, and why

Item 1 is the meter and comes first because the question "does cost reduction equal RMSD reduction" has no answer without it. Items 2 and 3 are cheap and each targets one named mechanism (over-confidence, contraction); together they either fix the shipped cost or eliminate the two easy explanations in a day. Item 4 is the only lever the record calls steep, and it needs items 2 and 3 done first so it does not learn the contraction as signal. Item 5 is the long shot that would settle whether the answer is recognisable at all.
