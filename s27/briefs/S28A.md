# S28 LANE A -- THE AMPLITUDE READOUT: A CVaR-VQE WHOSE OUTPUT IS A SIGNED COMBINATION

You are lane A. Read `s27/S28_CONTRACT.md` first. Your question is the sprint's largest swing.

## Why this, in the record's own numbers
`docs/FINDINGS.md` S10-5 (the bound ladder, all ORACLE): the uniform top-75 average emits at
2.954 A; the convex hull of the K = 500 pool emits at 0.853 A; the AFFINE span of the same 500
windows holds the native at 0.035 A and emits at 0.064 A. Every operator the project ships is
convex (non-negative weights that sum to one). S27 (`s27/REPORT.md` section 6) measured why
better rankers cannot help a convex average: correct ranking information reduces the diversity
the average needs. A signed combination is a different operator: it can cancel a component that
every member shares (the 68% common-mode error, S23 L9) instead of averaging it in.

The quantum state offers signed weights for free: real amplitudes psi_i of the deployed
real-amplitude RY/CNOT circuit are signed, and interference is the quantum notion the project
has never used. The hypothesis has two halves and you measure them separately:

- EXPRESSIVITY: the family of structures C(theta) = sum_i psi_i(theta) W_i / sum_i psi_i(theta)
  (affine normalisation; W_i the pool windows in the medoid frame the deployed average uses,
  `s24.d_harness.readout_uniform` / `s12.instrument.coordinate_average`) reachable by the deployed
  circuit (n = 9, depth 3, 27 parameters) contains structures far nearer the native than the
  convex average. Measured ORACLE: minimise the CA-RMSD to the native over theta (finite
  differences or the parameter-shift gradient of the RMSD; 5 starts). This is the family's
  ceiling, labelled ORACLE, not a result.
- RECOGNITION: a NATIVE-FREE objective finds those structures. The objective is the spine's:
  F(theta) = CVaR_alpha(E; p) - T H(p) + lam * S(C(theta)), where E = zrank of the shipped score
  over the pool (the deployed Hamiltonian, `s27.run_pool.channels_for(pdb)["DIS"]`), p = psi^2,
  and S(C) = the shipped distogram Bayes-risk of the EMITTED structure's own distance map
  (`s12.instrument.shipped_score(dg, pair_dists(C))`, native-free). lam = 0 is the deployed
  selector; lam > 0 is the new formulation. The readout is C(theta) at the optimum, projected
  through the production projection (built chain).
