# S28 LANE A2 -- IS THE OBJECTIVE LOCALLY INFORMATIVE AT THE PRODUCTION POINT? (conditional second wave)

Second-wave brief for lane A, to be run ONLY after the recognition arms (lam grid, classical
controls, untrained circuit) are on the built chain with lane D's verdict. Read the contract
with addendum 1 first.

## The new angle, stated up front
If the recognition arms come out far worse than production (the partial 43/126 rows in
S28-L17 show point-cloud values of 4 to 9 A against 3.05), the reading is that the shipped
distogram objective, given the freedom of signed weights, walks AWAY from the native. S15
measured the objective's global optimum (3.321 A, torsion space) and S8-9 its ranking of the
native among pool members (37th percentile). Nobody has measured the objective's LOCAL
behaviour at the pipeline's own output: does the first step of steepest descent on
S(C) from the production average move toward or away from the native, and at what step size
does the emitted structure start to degrade? This is the question a "trust region" around
production would need answered, and it is the mechanism statement the report needs: not "the
objective fails" but "the objective's gradient at the production point has cosine c with the
direction to the native, and a step of e A costs / gains d A".

## Design
- ORACLE DIAGNOSTIC (labelled): at the production point cloud C0 (DIS top-75 uniform
  average, medoid frame), the gradient g = dS/dC of the shipped Bayes-risk with respect to the
  CA coordinates (analytic through `pair_dists`, tested against finite differences), and the
  ORACLE direction u = (native aligned to C0) - C0. Report per target the cosine between -g
  and u (after removing the rigid-body components from both), its distribution over 126, the
  FAIL18 / 108 split, and the same cosine for the gradient of each S27 CA channel that has one
  (DISTPOT, CONTACT, ENV, EXVOL, CAGEO, RG_LAW) so the distogram is placed among them.
- DEPLOYABLE ARM (native-free): C(e) = project(C0 - e g / |g|) for e in {0.1, 0.3, 1.0} A of
  RMS displacement, the built chain, paired against production; controls: a random direction
  of the same displacement (8 draws, mean and best-of-8 as an order statistic), and the
  SAME step restricted to the circuit family (the signed-amplitude family of S28A, one Adam
  step from the production-reproducing theta, so the family's inductive bias is tested at the
  local scale where lam = 0 sits). Falsifier: some e beats production on the built chain beyond
  its MDE with the fold CI excluding zero on 5/5 folds and beats the random-direction control
  beyond MDE. Registered prior: cosine near zero or negative on most targets (the distogram's
  errors are common-mode with the pool's, S23 L9); the step degrades at every e; the random
  direction degrades equally. If the cosine is positive on the 108 and negative on FAIL18, say
  so: that is the sequence-conditioning sign flip (memory: blind pipeline beats shipped on
  FAIL18) seen from the objective's side.
- Cost: classical, seconds per target; the built chain 3 s per target per arm (4 arms x 126
  plus 8 random x 126 on the point cloud only, built chain for the mean random arm).
