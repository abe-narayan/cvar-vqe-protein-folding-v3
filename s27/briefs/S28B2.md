# S28 LANE B2 -- A HOPPING GRAPH WITH A SPREAD SPECTRUM (conditional second wave)

Second-wave brief for lane B, to be run ONLY if (a) the F1 to F4 endpoint arms of S28B are
null or worse on the built chain and (b) lane D's check confirms S28-L8b's mechanism (the
Gaussian similarity graph is near rank one, one eigenvalue 1.0 and the next 0.11 to 0.14, so
the hopping term is the squared overlap with one near-uniform vector). Read the contract with
addendum 1 first.

## The new angle, stated up front
S28-L8b found that the hopping term is invisible to the circuit's gradient at n = 9 BECAUSE the
graph is a typicality projector, not because it is off-diagonal (the diagonal same-spectrum
control decays at the same rate on the padding-free registers, S28-L11). That is a statement
about the graph's SPECTRUM. A graph whose spectrum is spread (many eigenvalues of comparable
size) is a different Hamiltonian in the one respect the mechanism names, and it is the only
follow-up the mechanism licenses. Anything else (finer J, other sigma) is a cosmetic variant
and is not run.

## Design
- Graph: the symmetric k-nearest-neighbour graph on the same pairwise CA-RMSD (k in {5, 10}),
  degree-normalised (D^-1/2 A D^-1/2) so the spectral norm is 1 and the spectrum is that of a
  sparse graph (state lambda_2 / lambda_1 and the participation ratio of the top eigenvector
  per target, against the Gaussian graph's 0.11 to 0.14 and 0.88 to 0.97). One k is the arm,
  the other is its replication; no k grid beyond two.
- Everything else as S28B: F = CVaR - T H - J <psi|A|psi>, J in {0.3, 1, 3} (three rungs, the
  0.1 rung dropped as invisible), the three readouts, PERM and RAND controls, seed 0 and 1.
- The quantum-side measurement first (it does not depend on RMSD): the hop-only gradient
  variance at n = 4..9 for the kNN graph beside the Gaussian graph's -1.7 to -1.8 per qubit,
  and the sign coherence / share of the same-sign bound the circuit collects at each J. The
  falsifier for "the spectrum was the mechanism": the kNN hop-only slope is shallower than
  -1.0 per qubit and the circuit collects more than half of its same-sign bound at J = 1
  (Gaussian: 4% at J = 1, 33% at J = 3, S28-L11).
- Endpoint: the built chain, J = 0 and each J, paired against PRODUCTION (S28-L2(a)) and
  against J = 0, with PERM and RAND, both seeds, FAIL18 / 108, best-J priced as an order
  statistic. Registered prior for the endpoint: WORSE or null (delocalising over a similar
  cluster is the consistency mechanism S27 section 6 measured as harmful); the value of the
  run is the trainability half.
