# S28 LANE C -- WHERE THE ERROR LIVES: FAIL18 DETECTION WITH NEW INPUTS, AND A NON-AVERAGING READOUT FOR RANKING INFORMATION

You are lane C, the classical accuracy lane. Read `s27/S28_CONTRACT.md` first.

## Why this, in the record's own numbers
FAIL18 (18 of 126 targets) sits at 5.83 A against 2.58 A for the other 108 (`s27/results/
strata.json`) and carries most of the mean. S27 found that every alternative Hamiltonian helps on
FAIL18 by 0.1 to 0.4 A and hurts or is null on the 108 (DIS+DISTPOT -0.191 / +0.010; DIS+ENV
-0.401 / +0.064; DISTPOT alone -0.364 / +0.237; even CONS, LEG and AMB help on FAIL18). A
native-free switch would be worth up to 0.03 to 0.06 A on the mean. Seven router constructions
have failed to find one (S22 L7, S23 L7, S26 L110/L115: eighteen routers, null-to-harmful; the
S23 scale signal is unreachable in principle, S23 L6). The new angle, stated up front: the
inputs. No previous router saw the pool's own statistical-potential distribution or its
agreement with the distogram. S27's channels give, per target and native-free: the spread and
skew of DISTPOT / ENV / CONTACT over the pool, their rank correlation with DIS, the overlap of
their top-75 with DIS's, the distogram's mean per-pair entropy, the pool's contact-frequency
entropy, the top-75's mean pairwise RMSD. If those do not separate FAIL18 either, the detector
question is closed on a genuinely new feature class.

The second question comes from S27 section 6: ranking information hurts an averaging readout.
CONS has partial rho +0.25 with the ORACLE candidate RMSD given DIS and is the worst complement.
Is there a readout that consumes ranking information WITHOUT losing the average's variance
reduction? The candidates, each native-free: (a) medoid-of-top-75-under-CONS averaged with its
k nearest neighbours inside the top-75 (k in {10, 20, 40}); (b) a two-stage average: DIS top-75,
then drop only the members whose CONS rank is in the worst decile (an outlier trim, not a
consensus selection; S23 L5 trimmed by geometry, not by a ranker); (c) a diversity-preserving
weighted average: weights w_i proportional to exp(-beta zrank(CONS)_i) times a repulsion that
penalises near-duplicate members (a determinantal-style rescaling). Prior: (a) and (c) null to
worse, (b) null; the value is closing the "consumer of ranking information" question that S27
opened.

## Pre-register (`s27/PREREG_S28_C.md`) before any endpoint number
Part 1, the detector. Label: FAIL18 membership (ORACLE, used only as the training label inside
nested leave-fold-out CV and never at inference). Features: the list above plus S22/S23's
controls (length, retrieval-score entropy, distogram entropy). Models: a ridge logistic
classifier and a one-threshold rule per feature, nested. Metric: held-out AUROC against a
500-draw label-permutation null; then the endpoint of the switched arm (DIS on predicted-OK,
DIS+DISTPOT or DIS+ENV on predicted-FAIL) on the built chain, paired against production.
Falsifier: held-out AUROC above the permutation null's 95th percentile AND the switched arm
beating production on the built chain beyond its MDE with the fold CI excluding zero on 5/5
folds. Prior: null at the AUROC step.
Part 2, the readouts (a) to (c), each on the point cloud and the built chain, paired against
production, with a matched-random control (the same trim or weighting applied with a permuted
CONS), the random-75 null, and `best_of_k_within` on any grid (k, beta). Falsifier as always.
